An assistant with a face

Not a chat widget in the corner. A small creature who lives on the page, knows what Vishnu has built, and answers only from a fixed knowledge base — thirty-five entries, no model weights, no server. Everything you type stays in this tab.

The buddy is the interface: it blinks, watches your cursor, thinks visibly, and shrugs when it does not know something. Retrieval is lexical scoring over hand-written facts, so it cannot invent a metric — the failure mode is "I don't know", which is the one you want.
